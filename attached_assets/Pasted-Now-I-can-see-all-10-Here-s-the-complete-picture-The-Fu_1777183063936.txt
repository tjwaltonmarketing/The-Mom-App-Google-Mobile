Honest Assessment
The good news: Screens 8, 9, and 10 are excellent. The two priming screens back to back before the paywall is actually a smart psychological sequence — screen 8 removes payment anxiety, screen 9 removes cancellation anxiety. That's textbook conversion optimization and most apps don't do it this well.
The problem: You have 5 consecutive feature education screens (3-7) before you get to the priming sequence. That's where people are dropping off. By the time they reach screen 8 most of them are already gone.

My Recommended Cut
Drop from 10 to 6 screens:
KeepCut✅ Screen 1 — Welcome❌ Screen 2 — Overview (redundant with welcome)✅ Screen 6 — AI Assistant❌ Screen 3 — Smart Calendar✅ Screen 7 — Built for Whole Family❌ Screen 4 — Smart Task Management✅ Screen 8 — Priming A❌ Screen 5 — Meal Planning✅ Screen 9 — Priming B✅ Screen 10 — Paywall

Why Keep AI Assistant and Built for Whole Family
AI Assistant is your killer feature and the biggest differentiator — it earns its screen.
Built for the Whole Family is important because it's the core value proposition — this isn't just a personal organizer, it's a family system. That distinction justifies the subscription price and should be shown before the paywall.
Calendar, Tasks, and Meal Planning are all features users will discover naturally once they're in the app. They don't need to be sold on each one individually before they've even started a trial.
