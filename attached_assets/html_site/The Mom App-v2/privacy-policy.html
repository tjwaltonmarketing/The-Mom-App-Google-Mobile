Privacy Policy

The Mom App™

Last updated: 1/1/2026

1. Overview

Your privacy matters to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use The Mom App.

2. Information We Collect

a) Information You Provide

Name

Email address

Family or household details

Notes, tasks, calendar entries

Voice recordings (if enabled)

b) Automatically Collected Information

Device type

App usage data

Log information

IP address (for security)

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

Operate and improve the App

Sync family features

Provide AI and voice functionality

Communicate with you

Maintain security and prevent abuse

4. Voice & AI Data

Voice recordings and AI interactions:

Are used only to provide App functionality

Are not sold

May be processed by secure third-party services

5. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal data.

We may share information only:

With service providers necessary to operate the App

If required by law

To protect safety, rights, or security

6. Data Security

We implement reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect your data. However, no system is 100% secure.

7. Children’s Privacy

The App is not intended for children under 10 without parental involvement. Parents control family access and content.

8. Your Choices

You may:

Update your account information

Delete your account

Request data access or deletion

Contact us at themomapp.us@gmail.com.

9. Third-Party Services

The App may integrate with third-party tools (e.g., analytics, payments). Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy. Updates will be posted in the App or on our website.
