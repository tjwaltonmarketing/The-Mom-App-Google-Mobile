Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions? We've got answers.

  • How does the AI voice assistant work?

    Simply speak naturally to the app, and our AI will understand your request and add it to the right place. For example, say "Add soccer practice Thursday at 4pm" and it will automatically create a calendar event with reminders. No special commands needed!

  • Can I use this app with my partner and kids?

    Absolutely! The Family plan supports up to 6 family members. You can share calendars, assign tasks, and keep everyone synced. Each family member gets their own login and can have private or shared items.

  • Is my data secure and private?

    Yes. We use military-grade encryption for all your data, especially for the password vault. Your information is never shared with third parties. You control what's shared with family members and what stays private.

  • Can I try it before paying?

    Yes! We offer a 14-day free trial with full access to all features. No credit card required to start. You can cancel anytime during the trial with no charges.

  • Does the meal planning work for dietary restrictions?

    Yes! You can set dietary preferences, allergies, and restrictions in your profile. The AI will only suggest meals that fit your family's needs and will automatically filter out ingredients you can't have.

  • What happens if I cancel my subscription?

    You can cancel anytime from your account settings. You'll continue to have access until the end of your billing period. Your data is securely stored for 90 days if you decide to come back, then permanently deleted if you don't.

  • Is this app available on both iOS and Android?

    Yes! The Mom App is available on both the App Store (iOS) and Google Play (Android). Your data syncs seamlessly across all devices, so you can access it from your phone or tablet.

  • How do child accounts work if my child is too young for a mobile device? Do I need
    to pay for each child?

    Child accounts are unlimited and free with any active parent subscription for kids who are too young to have their own mobile device. Here’s how it works:

    No login required. Children do not need their own account or device.

    Unlimited children. Add as many kids as you need at no extra cost.

    Printable task lists. Each child gets a personalized, kid friendly task list you can print and hang on the fridge, wall, or bedroom door.

    Weekly updates. Print fresh task lists each week with their assigned chores and responsibilities.

    Parent managed. Everything is controlled from your parent account.

    This setup keeps things simple for families. Younger kids can clearly see their tasks without needing a phone, and parents manage everything from one place without juggling multiple logins or paying per child.
