A fuzzy or faded app icon on the App Store is almost always caused by using a low-resolution image, incorrect image format, or neglecting the new "Liquid Glass" design standards in iOS 26.

Here is a checklist of fixes based on the latest 2026 App Store requirements to make your icon look crisp:
1. Fix Resolution and Dimensions
Use 1024 x 1024 px: The master app icon must be exactly 1024 x 1024 pixels.
Avoid Upscaling: Ensure the original design was created at this size. Stretching a smaller image (e.g., 512x512) will create a blurry or pixelated result.
Use High Resolution: Use high-resolution PNG, vector (SVG, EPS, AI), or PDF files.

2. Adhere to Apple’s 2026 "Liquid Glass" Guidelines 
As of iOS 26, Apple uses a "Liquid Glass" design language that adds depth, shadows, and transparency. 
Layer Your Artwork: Instead of a single flattened image, provide layered artwork (using Apple's Icon Composer) to ensure the icon looks sharp and interacts with light correctly, rather than appearing blurry.
Remove Pre-applied Effects: Do not add your own shadows or rounded corners. Apple automatically applies a "squircle" mask and system-level effects.
Avoid Transparency: Your icon must be fully opaque. Do not leave empty (transparent) areas in the PNG, as they can cause rendering issues or display as black.

3. Check Design and Color
Keep it Simple: Complex, detailed logos look messy at small sizes (like 29x29 for Settings). Focus on a bold, simple symbol.
High Contrast: Use strong contrast between foreground and background for better visibility.
Check Safe Zones: Ensure your logo isn't too close to the edge, or it may be cut off by the rounded corner mask.

4. Technical Checklist
Re-upload to App Store Connect: Sometimes an old, low-res image is cached. Re-uploading a fresh 1024x1024 png file usually fixes this.
Clear Local Cache: Delete the app from your device and re-download it to check the actual quality, as the simulator or old builds may show cached, low-quality icons.
